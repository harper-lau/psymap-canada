About this map

Find your way into psychology.

Psychology is the scientific study of mind and behaviour—how people think, feel, learn, connect, and change.

Psychology Map Canada was created to make that wide field easier to explore. It brings schools, research areas, professors, courses, and career paths into one calm, student-friendly place.

Built for students who are curious before they know exactly what to search.
